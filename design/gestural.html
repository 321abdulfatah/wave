The fivehand shapes

A visitor has to guess the vocabulary in under a second, in the rain, holding a box. So there are five shapes, they are large, and they are already what people do at doors. Print this plate and pin it beside the bell.

  1. 01

    Thumbs up

    Yes

    Agrees with whatever the door just asked. The workhorse of the set.

  2. 02

    Open palm

    Wait

    Holds the conversation open while the visitor finds a parcel or a name.

  3. 03

    Wave

    Hello, I am a person

    Opens the exchange and separates a caller from a passing cat.

  4. 04

    Point down

    Leaving it here

    Marks the spot. WAVE captures the frame as proof of placement.

  5. 05

    Closed fist

    No

    Declines. The door thanks them and the event closes without entry.
